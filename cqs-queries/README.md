## Competency Questions

SemRepo-V2 is evaluated against 40 Competency Questions (CQs) designed to capture representative information needs in software repository analytics. The CQs were formulated and reviewed by domain experts to reflect concrete information needs, particularly those of researchers in scholarly analytics, research software engineers, and software maintainers.

The complete set of 40 CQs along with their corresponding SPARQL queries are available in this directory.
